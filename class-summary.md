# Class Summary: Walking and Boleos

## Components of the Walk

1. **Project:** Move the free leg away from the base, ending on balance.
2. **Push:** Use the muscles of the standing leg to push into the step.
3. **Pull:** Use the muscles of the new standing leg to pull into the next projection.

## Walking Technique and Exercises

### Leg Motion: Band Exercise

Place a band around the knee. Push against the band when projecting backward, and let it pull the knee forward when projecting forward.

The main takeaway is to **“split the thighs.”**

- **Moving backward:** The free knee straightens while the front leg bends.
- **Moving forward:** The free knee bends while the back leg straightens.

### Spine Opposite the Leg

The leg has weight and can pull us off balance. When projecting, keep the spine on the opposite side of the projected leg to counterbalance it.

Picture a scale or a seesaw: the projected leg is on one side, and the spine is on the other. Place the spine where it will help balance the next step.

### Balanced Projection: Step and Tap Exercise

An important concept in tango is **being on balance during the projection**.

1. Project the free leg.
2. Lift it briefly off the floor and tap it back down to check your balance.
3. Take a step, project again, and repeat the tap.

### Push Beyond the Projection

Using the juggling balls as markers, project to one ball, then push beyond that point toward the next ball.

Focus on **smoothing out the push**. Tango is about how smoothly we move together.

### Land in the Middle and Pull the Rest of the Way

Land in the middle, then use the new standing leg to pull the rest of the way. Using that leg to pull helps maintain balance and makes the movement smooth.

### Technique

Use the same technique from walking for boleos:

1. Stretch the leg backward, just as in a projection.
2. Bend the knee after the leg comes off the floor.
3. Let the leg come down before the knee comes forward.
4. Keep the spine forward to counterbalance the movement.

Focus on making the movement **as smooth as possible**, rather than as fast as possible. Try to let the leg be free by the end.
